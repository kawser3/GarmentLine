Notes

The seam here is a real gap rather than a colour change, so the mark sits on a photo or a coloured header without a background plate behind it.

Below 32px the stitches and the gap fall under a pixel and turn to mush. The 20 and 16 swatches above use the plain cut instead — keep both in the asset set and switch on size, the way an icon font ships separate optical sizes.

Stitches set after the panels land, so the sequence reads as joining then securing. Total run is about 1.3 seconds, which is at the top of what works for a login splash and too long for anything that fires on every navigation.
